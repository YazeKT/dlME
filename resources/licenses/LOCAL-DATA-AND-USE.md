# dlME local data and use

dlME 0.9.1 is an open beta. Download only material you own or have permission
to save. Supported-site listings do not grant rights to media or access.

Downloads remain in the selected folders on this PC. Download history,
settings, and diagnostic logs are stored locally in the app profile. History
can include source URLs, titles, file paths, and error details. Exported
diagnostics contain this information; review them before sharing.

Media requests connect to the sites you choose and their delivery networks.
The optional engine update check connects to GitHub. Thumbnail previews may
connect to the media site's image servers. dlME has no app telemetry service.

Browser-session access is optional. When enabled, yt-dlp reads the selected
browser session for that download. dlME stores the browser/profile selection,
not passwords or cookie contents.

Custom legal documents are copied into the app profile and displayed as plain
text. They are local additions and are not uploaded or automatically included
in a build for other users. Adding a custom document does not override the
licenses of third-party software.
